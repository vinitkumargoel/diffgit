# T11.9 — Secret scan surfaces

**Size:** M · **Depends on:** T11.8, T10.7 · Design §14.3 · Atlas tab 14

## Deliverables
- After every compute that has uncommitted layers, call `scanSecrets(generation)` (after stats
  complete; skip when no staged/unstaged files); store `secrets`.
- `SecretBanner` (danger level, count, `Show` scrolls to the first file and expands it, `Rules`
  opens a popover with the rule-set date and the allowlist syntax); sidebar row chip `secret`
  (`--chip-conflict` palette, after the XY code); in `DiffBody` the flagged line gets a 3 px `--danger`
  left rule and an inline trigger opening `SecretPopover` (rule, entropy, where, allowlist help,
  `Reveal`/`Mask`, `Copy rotate checklist`); findings masked everywhere including the card's "Copy
  report" and T11.10 exports (gate).
- Palette action `Re-scan for secrets`.

## Tests
- Banner and chip appear on the recorded `secrets` data, absent on clean; popover reveal/mask; masked in
  copy; scan is not called for committed-only diffs; a11y.
